01 / CORE POLICIES

The short version.

These four commitments describe the boundaries of the WIT Research & Analysis prototype.

shield
Privacy

Session-first processing

Images uploaded for analysis are processed only to generate the immediate result and are not retained beyond the session. No account or login is required. Patient-detail fields entered on a report are used only to populate that downloaded PDF and are not transmitted or stored elsewhere.

menu_book
Terms of research

An educational prototype

This platform is provided for research and educational use only. It is not a certified medical device and must not be used as the basis for an actual clinical or treatment decision. Users are responsible for verifying any output with a qualified professional.

account_balance
Institutional guidelines

Academic oversight

This work is conducted under the academic guidance of Walchand Institute of Technology, Solapur. Any extension toward real clinical use would require institutional review, regulatory clearance, and clinical validation beyond this platform's scope.

balance
Ethics statement

Inspectable research

The models were trained exclusively on publicly available, de-identified research datasets. No real patient data was used in training or evaluation. Every prediction is paired with a Grad-CAM visualization so model reasoning stays inspectable.

database
Data boundary

No account required

The detection workflow is designed for a local research session. Uploads and report details are not presented as a personal record or long-term patient file.

person_search
Human responsibility

Context over automation

Use the model output as a prompt for learning and investigation. A qualified professional remains responsible for any real-world interpretation or decision.

02 / DATA LIFECYCLE

What happens in a session.

01

You choose an image

The selected file is sent to the matching research model for one analysis request.

02

The model returns context

The result includes a predicted class, probabilities, confidence, and available Grad-CAM output.

03

You decide what to keep

Optional details are used in a local PDF report; the platform does not create an account record.

warning

Important notice / research use only

This platform is not a certified medical device and should not be used for diagnosis, treatment, triage, or other clinical decisions.
