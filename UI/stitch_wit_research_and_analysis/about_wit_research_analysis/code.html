Institution

Walchand Institute of Technology

WIT Research & Analysis is developed at Walchand Institute of Technology (WIT), Solapur, established in 1983 and named after industrialist Seth Walchand Hirachand. WIT is the oldest engineering college in Solapur and its first autonomous institute, accredited NAAC A+ and affiliated with Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Solapur University.

An academic starting point

The platform is designed to support learning, experimentation, and transparent discussion around medical image analysis. It makes the model output easier to examine without presenting it as a clinical decision.
