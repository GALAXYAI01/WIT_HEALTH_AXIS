01 / IMAGE REQUIREMENTS

Start with the right sample.

The models expect microscopy imagery from their corresponding research pathway. Use a clear image and choose the module that matches its source.

02 / READING RESULTS

Three views, one workflow.

After analysis, move through the result tabs deliberately. Each view answers a different research question.

01

Summary

Review the predicted class, confidence, and any flagged result indicator.

02

All probabilities

Compare the probability assigned to every available class instead of reading one number in isolation.

03

Grad-CAM overlay

Inspect the heatmap of image regions that most influenced the model output.

Download Report

The PDF report includes the result, probabilities, patient or sample details, and available original and Grad-CAM images.

Keep a record

Use sample IDs and collection dates consistently when comparing results across a research exercise.

warning

Research and educational use only

This platform is not a substitute for professional diagnosis. Always confirm findings with a qualified professional, and never use a model output as the sole basis for a clinical or treatment decision.
